Portfolio overview & contact
about this work

Connecting Environmental Focus with Industry-Ready Analytics

Across these projects, the throughline is simple: pair an earth-first mindset with practical analytics. Datasets range from satellite-derived climate series and ecological observations to transaction streams and visitor logs. Tools range from Python and Spark to Tableau, but the goals stay the same — understand the system, quantify risk or impact, and communicate the results so they’re usable.

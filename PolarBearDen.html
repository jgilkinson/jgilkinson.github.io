Data pipeline

The notebook ingests multiple CSV and Excel files, cleans and renames columns (for example standardizing Spring_year to Year Discovered), and writes harmonized tables into SQLite databases for analysis and visualization.

Interpretation
Ecology

The final maps support the working hypothesis: den locations show a clear movement away from sea ice toward the mainland, consistent with increased structural insecurity of coastal ice as temperatures rise. The analysis is careful not to claim causality, but it highlights an urgent signal for Arctic habitat stability.

  • Observed den drift correlates with rising average temperatures
  • Patterns align with broader literature on sea ice loss
  • Data gaps and observational bias remain important caveats

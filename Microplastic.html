Methods
AI & modeling

The system combines deep learning for object detection with temporal modeling and synthetic data generation to capture microplastics in a wide range of real-world conditions.

  • YOLOv5 / ResNet for detection and classification
  • Neural networks + Hidden Markov Models for spatio-temporal trends
  • GANs to generate synthetic training images and reduce data gaps

Platform & Data
Crowdsourcing

A mobile and web platform lets citizens capture microplastics photos, automatically attach metadata, and receive feedback — transforming millions of smartphones into a distributed sensor network.

  • Crowdsourced water, soil, and biological sample photos
  • Metadata: timestamps, GPS, device details, environmental context
  • Lab-verified ground truth datasets for training & validation
Ethics & Equity
Governance

The design emphasizes informed consent, privacy protections, and responsible communication — ensuring that high-resolution pollution maps inform action without stigmatizing communities.

  • Clear consent on image use and data retention
  • Location anonymization & sensitive content filtering
  • Open, explainable models and peer-reviewed methods
  • Careful framing to avoid blaming affected regions

Pipeline & impact

From Citizen Photos to Policy-Ready Contamination Maps

Inspired by the microplastics detection pipeline and heatmap visualizations in the white paper, the platform routes crowdsourced photos through data preparation, AI/ML modeling, and geo-analytics to deliver contamination probability maps and hotspot alerts for policymakers and communities.
